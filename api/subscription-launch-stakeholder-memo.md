# Stakeholder Update: Recommendation to Delay Subscription Launch

## Summary

We recommend delaying the subscription service launch by **3 weeks** to address a high-severity security issue in our payment system. While no customers have been affected and there is no evidence of active exploitation, security experts consider this type of issue likely to be targeted once it becomes publicly known. Delaying the launch now significantly reduces the risk of financial loss, customer impact, and reputational damage after release.

---

## Why This Matters

The issue affects how our payment system verifies payment requests.

In simple terms, the system currently accepts a digital payment receipt that could be copied and reused by an attacker to make unauthorized charges. Think of it like a concert ticket that can be photocopied—if the venue cannot tell the original from the copy, someone could enter multiple times using the same ticket.

Although nobody has attempted this against our platform yet, similar vulnerabilities at other companies have been exploited shortly after they became publicly known.

---

## Business Impact

- Subscription launch will move **3 weeks** later than planned.
- Engineering resources will focus on resolving the security issue before adding new customer-facing features.
- Marketing and product launch timelines will need to shift accordingly.
- Customers receive a more secure and trustworthy product at launch.
- The company reduces the risk of fraudulent payments, customer refunds, regulatory issues, and brand damage.

---

## Why We Recommend Delaying

Launching on schedule would mean knowingly releasing a product with a significant security weakness.

Delaying by three weeks allows us to:

- Remove the security risk before customers use the new feature.
- Protect customer payment information and company reputation.
- Avoid significantly higher costs that could result from responding to a security incident after launch.

---

## Recommendation

We recommend approving a **3-week launch delay** so the engineering team can complete the required security work before releasing the subscription service.

### Requested Actions

- Approve moving the launch date by three weeks.
- Update the product and marketing launch schedule.
- Schedule a 30-minute alignment meeting this week so Product, Marketing, Security, and Engineering can coordinate external communication and the revised release timeline.

Thank you for your support as we prioritise customer trust and long-term business reliability.
